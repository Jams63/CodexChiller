Free Entry Camping OK Family Friendly Turtle Beach
Fuwairit Beach
📍 Al Shamal · ~120 km north of Doha · ~1.5 hr drive

Qatar's most-loved wild beach. Wide sandy shores, crystal Gulf water, and zero crowds. Sea turtles nest here in summer. Overnight family camping is a local tradition.

  • Directions: Al Shamal Road (Route 1) north → exit at Fuwairit village sign → follow coastal track to beach.
  • Completely free, no gate, no ticket. Open 24 hours.
  • No facilities — bring your own grill, charcoal, water & tent.
  • Best Oct–Apr. 4×4 helpful on soft sand closer to shore.

Simaisma North Beach
📍 Al Daayen · ~30 km north of Doha · ~25 min drive

The closest proper beach to Doha for a quick family escape. Calm shallow water, shaded seating, on-site BBQ grills, toilets — everything sorted for a relaxed day out.

  • Directions: Al Khor Coastal Road north from Doha → exit at Simaisma signs → straight to beachfront.
  • Entry: ~50 QAR per car. BBQ grills and shaded loungers on-site.
  • Toilets every 300 m. Ample parking. Family park adjacent.
  • Shallow calm water — safe for toddlers and young children.

Free Camping OK BBQ Rock Formations
Zekreet Beach
📍 Al Shahaniya · ~80 km northwest of Doha · ~1 hr drive

Remote, dramatic, and unforgettable. Rugged rocky cliffs meet clear Gulf water. Qatar's famous "Film City" rock formations sit just minutes away — a bonus hike after your BBQ.

  • Directions: Dukhan Highway (Route 3) west from Doha → follow signs to Zekreet (Bir Zekrit) → bear right to coastline.
  • Completely free. No entry fee. Open 24 hours.
  • Bring all supplies: water, BBQ kit, tent, fuel. No facilities.
  • Rocky shore — wear sandals. 4×4 recommended off the main track.

Free Mangroves Camping OK Family Friendly
Al Thakira Beach (Purple Island)
📍 Al Khor · ~60 km north of Doha · ~45 min drive

Hidden behind dense mangroves, this quiet inlet near Al Khor glows purple at sunset. Flat open ground for BBQ and camping, birdsong all around, and kayaking through mangrove tunnels.

  • Directions: Al Khor Road north → into Al Khor city → follow "Al Thakira Mangroves" or "Purple Island" signs to the shore.
  • Completely free. Kayak rentals available nearby (~30–50 QAR/hr).
  • Flat open area — ideal for tents and BBQ beside the water.
  • Go at sunset for the famous purple-lit shallow water glow.
