1 Free Public
South · Mesaieed
Sealine Beach
📍 ~55 km south of Doha  ·  ~40 min

Qatar's most iconic camping spot — the only public beach where towering sand dunes crash right into the Gulf. Dune bashing, quad bikes, and the best bonfire nights in the country.

  • Directions: Al Wakrah Rd south → Mesaieed Rd → signs to Sealine / Khor Al Adaid.
  • Free entry. BBQ stands on-site ~150 QAR with charcoal. Lifeguards.
  • Standard car OK; 4×4 for dune access.

2 Free
South · Al Wakrah
Al Wakrah Family Beach
📍 ~25 km south of Doha  ·  ~25 min

The easiest south-of-Doha escape — free BBQ pits, playground, shaded gazebos, calm shallow water. Perfect for a spontaneous family evening without a long drive.

  • Directions: Al Wakrah Main Rd south → signs to Al Wakrah Corniche / Family Beach.
  • Fully free. BBQ pits, toilets, showers, shaded seating.
  • Calm water — safe for toddlers. Parking on-site.

3 ~50 QAR/car
East · Al Daayen
Simaisma North Beach
📍 ~30 km north of Doha  ·  ~25 min

The closest proper beach to Doha. On-site BBQ grills, shaded loungers, toilets, and shallow calm water. Perfect for families who want everything ready without driving far.

  • Directions: Al Khor Coastal Rd north → exit at Simaisma signs.
  • Entry ~50 QAR/car. BBQ grills, shaded seating, toilets on-site.
  • Shallow water — safe for toddlers.

North — Al Khor & Beyond
4 Free
North · Al Khor
Al Thakira Beach
Purple Island
📍 ~60 km north of Doha  ·  ~45 min

Hidden behind dense mangroves, this serene inlet glows purple at sunset. Kayak through mangrove tunnels, then BBQ on flat open ground beside the glassy water.

  • Directions: Al Khor Rd north → into Al Khor city → "Al Thakira Mangroves" signs.
  • Free. Kayak rentals ~30–50 QAR/hr. Flat camping area.
  • Go at sunset for the purple water glow.

5 Free
North · Al Shamal
Fuwairit Beach
Turtle Beach
📍 ~120 km north of Doha  ·  ~1.5 hr

Qatar's most-loved wild beach. Sandy, uncrowded, and completely free. Sea turtles nest here in summer. Overnight camping is a tradition — the sky is pitch-black and full of stars.

  • Directions: Al Shamal Rd (Route 1) north → exit at Fuwairit village sign.
  • Free, open 24 hr. No facilities — bring everything.
  • Best Oct–Apr. 4×4 helpful on soft sand.

6 Free
North · Ash Shamal
Al Ghariya Beach
📍 ~85 km north of Doha  ·  ~1.5 hr

Just 5 km past Fuwairit, Al Ghariya has BBQ pits, showers, toilets and lush green picnic areas alongside wild open beach. The fishermen's village nearby is a bonus explore.

  • Directions: Al Shamal Rd north past Fuwairit → Al Ghariya signs (~5 km further).
  • Free. Maintained section has BBQ pits, showers, toilets.
  • Shallow water. Adjacent open beach for wild camping.

7 Free · Hidden Gem
Far North · Al Shamal
Al Maroona Beach
French Beach · Exit 72
📍 ~94 km north of Doha  ·  ~1 hr 15 min

Locals call it "French Beach." Golden sand, transparent water, starfish at your feet. Most visitors never find it. Great for crabbing, caravan overnight stays, and total quiet.

  • Directions: Al Shamal Rd → Exit 72 east → right at Maroona Mosque → 6 km to beach.
  • Fully free. No facilities — bring everything including water.
  • Crabbing at low tide. Caravan-friendly flat ground.

West Coast
8 Free
West · Al Shahaniya
Zekreet Beach
Film City Rocks
📍 ~80 km west of Doha  ·  ~1 hr

Rocky cliffs, clear water, and Qatar's iconic limestone "Film City" formations a short walk away. Remote and dramatic — one of the most photogenic spots in the Gulf.

  • Directions: Dukhan Hwy (Route 3) west → signs to Zekreet / Bir Zekrit → right to coast.
  • Free, open 24 hr. No facilities — bring all supplies.
  • Rocky shore — wear sandals. 4×4 recommended off-track.

9 Free · BBQ Pits
West · Zekreet Peninsula
Ras Broog
Ras Abrouq · Mushroom Rocks
📍 ~80 km west of Doha  ·  ~1 hr

Wind-carved limestone mushroom rocks rise from powder-white sand — one of the most surreal landscapes in Qatar. BBQ pits on-site. Stars here are extraordinary.

  • Directions: Dukhan Hwy → Exit 72 → Zekreet Rd → follow Ras Abrouq / peninsula tip signs.
  • Free entry. BBQ pits on-site. No other facilities.
  • Standard car on main road. 4×4 near the beach.

10 Free · Best Sunsets
West · Dukhan
Dukhan Beach
📍 ~90 km west of Doha  ·  ~1 hr 15 min

Qatar's sunset capital. The western horizon burns amber and deep red over the Gulf — unlike anything on the east coast. Crystal water, fine sand, wide open camping space.

  • Directions: Dukhan Hwy (Route 3) all the way west → Dukhan city → beach / corniche signs.
  • Free, open 24 hr. Bring own BBQ — no facilities on open beach.
  • Time your arrival 1 hr before sunset.

11 Free Public Zone
South-West · Umm Bab
Al Kharaij Beach
Palm Tree Beach
📍 ~90 km west of Doha  ·  ~1 hr

Palm trees lining a calm shallow bay — rare for Qatar. The gated family section has BBQ stands, showers, toilets and a kids' play area. The free public zone is open with no time limits.

  • Directions: Dukhan Hwy west → Umm Bab exit → Al Kharaij Beach signs (stay on paved road).
  • Public zone: free. Gated section: small fee. BBQ stands, showers, play area.
  • 4×4 recommended — last section can be unpaved.
