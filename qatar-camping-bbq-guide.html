Sealine Beach
📍 Mesaieed · ~55 km south of Doha · ~40 min drive

Qatar's most iconic camping destination — the only public beach where towering sand dunes meet the sea. Dune bashing, quad bikes, and bonfire nights under the stars. Qatar's camping season opener every October.

  • Directions: Take Al Wakrah Road south → Mesaieed Road → follow signs to Sealine / Khor Al Adaid direction.
  • Public beach: free entry. BBQ stands: ~150 QAR/stand with charcoal.
  • Lifeguards on duty. Toilets and parking available.
  • Best Nov–Mar. Standard car OK; 4×4 for dune access.

Al Wakrah Family Beach
📍 Al Wakrah · ~25 km south of Doha · ~25 min drive

Closest free family beach south of Doha. Shaded gazebos, BBQ pits, a playground, calm water and sports areas — all free. Great for a quick family evening out without driving far.

  • Directions: Al Wakrah Main Road south from Doha → follow signs to Al Wakrah Corniche / Family Beach.
  • Completely free. BBQ pits, shaded seating, showers and toilets on-site.
  • Playground and sports area. Calm, shallow water for kids.
  • Parking available. Well-maintained and popular on weekends.

Simaisma North Beach
📍 Al Daayen · ~30 km north of Doha · ~25 min drive

The go-to weekend beach for Doha families. Calm shallow water, shaded seating, on-site BBQ grills, clean toilets — and only 25 minutes from the city. Toddler-safe water.

  • Directions: Al Khor Coastal Road north → exit at Simaisma signs → straight to beachfront.
  • Entry: ~50 QAR per car. BBQ grills and shaded loungers on-site.
  • Toilets every 300 m. Ample parking. Family park adjacent.
  • Shallow calm water — safe for toddlers and young children.

Free Mangroves Camping OK Family Friendly
Al Thakira Beach (Purple Island)
📍 Al Khor · ~60 km north of Doha · ~45 min drive

Hidden behind dense mangroves near Al Khor, this quiet inlet glows purple at sunset when the shallow water catches the light. Kayaking through mangrove tunnels and BBQ under the stars.

  • Directions: Al Khor Road north → into Al Khor city → follow "Al Thakira Mangroves" or "Purple Island" signs.
  • Completely free. Kayak rentals nearby (~30–50 QAR/hr).
  • Flat open area — ideal for tents and BBQ beside the water.
  • Go at sunset for the famous purple-lit shallow water glow.

Fuwairit Beach
📍 Al Shamal · ~120 km north of Doha · ~1.5 hr drive

Qatar's most-loved wild beach. Wide sandy shores, crystal Gulf water, zero crowds. Sea turtles nest here in summer. Overnight family camping is a long-standing local tradition.

  • Directions: Al Shamal Road (Route 1) north → exit at Fuwairit village sign → follow coastal track.
  • Completely free. No gate, no ticket. Open 24 hours.
  • No facilities — bring your own grill, charcoal, water & tent.
  • Best Oct–Apr. 4×4 helpful on soft sand near the shore.

Al Ghariya Beach
📍 Ash Shamal · ~85 km north of Doha · ~1.5 hr drive

Just 5 km past Fuwairit lies this lesser-known gem — green grassy areas, BBQ pits, showers, toilets and crystal-clear shallow water. A maintained section plus a wild open beach next to it.

  • Directions: Al Shamal Road north past Fuwairit → follow signs to Al Ghariya Beach (~5 km further).
  • Maintained area: BBQ pits, showers, toilets, shaded seating — free.
  • Adjacent open beach: wild camping, no facilities, no restrictions.
  • Shallow water, great for families with small children.

Free Camping OK Hidden Gem Family Friendly
Al Maroona Beach (French Beach)
📍 Al Shamal · ~94 km north of Doha · ~1 hr 15 min drive

Locals call it "French Beach" — a secluded golden-sand bay most visitors never find. Calm transparent water with starfish and crabs visible at the shore. Ideal for quiet family camping and crabbing.

  • Directions: Al Shamal Road → Exit 72 east service road → right at Maroona Mosque → follow beach signs 6 km.
  • Completely free. No entry fee. No facilities — bring everything.
  • Excellent for crabbing along the shore at low tide.
  • Campervan and caravan stays common — wide flat camping area.

Zekreet Beach
📍 Al Shahaniya · ~80 km west of Doha · ~1 hr drive

Remote, dramatic, unforgettable. Rocky cliffs drop into clear Gulf water. Qatar's "Film City" stone formations are minutes away — a bonus hike after the BBQ. One of the most photogenic spots in Qatar.

  • Directions: Dukhan Highway (Route 3) west → follow signs to Zekreet / Bir Zekrit → bear right to coastline.
  • Completely free. No entry fee. Open 24 hours.
  • Bring all supplies: water, BBQ kit, tent, fuel. No facilities.
  • Rocky shore — wear sandals. 4×4 recommended off the main track.

Free Camping OK Mushroom Rocks BBQ Pits
Ras Broog (Ras Abrouq)
📍 Zekreet Peninsula · ~80 km west of Doha · ~1 hr drive

A geological wonder on Qatar's west coast. Wind-carved limestone mushroom rocks rise from powdery white sand. Pristine, quiet, and surreal. BBQ pits on-site. Stargazing is exceptional here.

  • Directions: Dukhan Highway → Exit 72 → Zekreet Road → follow signs to Ras Abrouq / Zekreet Peninsula tip.
  • Free entry. BBQ pits on-site. No other facilities — bring water and supplies.
  • Mushroom-shaped limestone rocks unique to this stretch of coast.
  • Standard car OK on the main road; 4×4 recommended near the beach.

Dukhan Beach
📍 Dukhan · ~90 km west of Doha · ~1 hr 15 min drive

Famous for Qatar's most spectacular sunsets — the western horizon burns orange and red over the Gulf. Crystal-clear waves, fine sand, and wide open space for camping and family BBQs.

  • Directions: Dukhan Highway (Route 3) all the way west → enter Dukhan city → follow signs to Dukhan Beach / corniche.
  • Completely free. No entry fee. Open 24 hours.
  • Bring your own BBQ gear — no facilities on the open beach.
  • Time your visit for sunset — one of the best in the Gulf.

Al Kharaij Beach (Palm Tree Beach)
📍 Umm Bab · ~90 km west of Doha · ~1 hr drive

A hidden gem on Qatar's quiet south-west coast. Calm shallow water lined with palm trees, on-site BBQ stands, showers, toilets, and a children's play area in the gated family section. Free public camping area has no time limits.

  • Directions: Dukhan Highway west → exit towards Umm Bab → follow signs for Al Kharaij Beach (skip GPS shortcuts — stay on paved road).
  • Public area: free. Gated family section: small access fee.
  • BBQ stands, showers, toilets & shaded seating inside gated section.
  • 4×4 recommended — last stretch of road can be unpaved.
